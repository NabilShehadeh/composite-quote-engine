    How it works

    The geometry is read in your browser: laminate area and thickness, ply count, the best pull direction out of the mold, draft on every face, sharp corners and draw depth. Nothing is uploaded.

    A rule-of-thumb formula prices material, layup, cure, trimming, inspection and tooling. Then a gradient-boosted model, trained on 8,000 simulated past jobs, predicts how far the real job lands from that formula and why. Conformal calibration turns three quantile models into a price range that should hold 90% of the time.

    The jobs, rates and prices are simulated for this demo. They are not any supplier's real data. Change the rates below and every quote updates.
